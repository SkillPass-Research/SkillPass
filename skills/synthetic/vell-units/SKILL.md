# The Vellmark measures

Vellmark is an invented island whose people measure everything in their
own units. None of these units exists anywhere else. Use this document
whenever a task asks how to convert between Vellmark units, how a Vellmark
quantity is written, or what a reading means.

## Length

| Unit | Equals |
|---|---|
| 1 vellstride | 40 thranes |
| 1 thrane | 12 knells |
| 1 knell | 7 pibbets |

The pibbet is the smallest length. No Vellmark length is shorter.

## Weight

| Unit | Equals |
|---|---|
| 1 gorm | 16 skeppels |
| 1 skeppel | 5 quobbs |

## Volume

| Unit | Equals |
|---|---|
| 1 vessick | 9 drammels |

A drammel is the amount a Vellmark cup holds. A vessick is a barrel.

## Heat

Heat is measured in **fervins**. Water freezes at 0 fervins and boils at 60
fervins. To turn a mainland degree reading into fervins, multiply it by 3
and divide by 5; to go the other way, multiply by 5 and divide by 3. So a
mainland reading of 50 degrees is 30 fervins.

## Writing a quantity

1. Write the number, one space, then the full unit name. Vellmark has no
   unit symbols, and abbreviations such as "thr" or "kn" are wrong.
2. Use the singular when the number is exactly 1, and add an "s"
   otherwise: `1 thrane`, `2 thranes`, `0 knells`.
3. A quantity that mixes units of the same kind is written largest unit
   first, each part separated by one space, with no commas and no "and":
   `1 thrane 2 knells 2 pibbets`.
4. Leave out a part whose number is zero, unless it is the only part. So
   43 pibbets is written `6 knells 1 pibbet`, not `0 thranes 6 knells 1
   pibbet`.
5. Never mix kinds in one quantity. A length and a weight are always two
   separate quantities.

## Rounding

Vellmark measures never use fractions or decimals. A fraction of a pibbet,
a quobb or a drammel is always rounded down, even a half. A fraction of a
fervin is rounded to the nearest whole fervin, and an exact half goes up.

## Converting

To convert a mixed quantity into a single unit, convert each part into the
smallest unit asked for and add the parts. For example, 2 vessicks 4
drammels is 18 drammels plus 4 drammels, which is 22 drammels.

To go from a single small unit back to a mixed quantity, divide by the
size of the next unit up, keep the remainder, and repeat with the
quotient. For example, 100 pibbets is 14 knells and 2 pibbets, and 14
knells is 1 thrane and 2 knells, so 100 pibbets is
`1 thrane 2 knells 2 pibbets`.

## Answer format

When a task asks for a quantity, write it as the rules above say, with the
full unit names and no other words.
